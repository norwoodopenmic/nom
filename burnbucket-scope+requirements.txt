******* WHY BURNBUCKET *********
this is a side project that can possibly give solace to anyone who is suffering and is stuck and wants to move forward

******* BURNBUCKET EXPLAINED *********
In real life you can write something down and burn it. In the computer you can write something down and release the writing back into memory.
