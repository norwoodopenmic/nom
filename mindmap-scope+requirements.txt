******* MINDMAP EXPLAINED *********
Let's say I need to remember that I have work calls today at 10 AM, 11 AM and 2 PM.
My mindmap would look like this:
David  ->  Work -> Calls -> 10 AM -> 11 AM -> 2 PM

At the start of each day I look at my work calendar and fill out my mind map for the day. 

My mindmap might also  have something like
David -> Logan -> Meds -> 8 AM

By linking every task back to me through things like Logan or Work I am maintaing the connection of the task to me. Something I do that isn't good is that I can literally lose myself in a task, the mindmap helps me find my way back to me.
