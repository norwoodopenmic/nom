My first or second day in treatment a certain clinician tried to do the burn bucket with me because another clinician was out dealing with some stuff. The clinician said write down something meaningful to you and burn it. I was like wait a second that doesn't sound right... so I wrote some beautiful processing down and then I kept it. I watched others burn their notes in the parking lot of Rockland.

Later on as the weeks went on the burn bucket exercise returned and I would write down like a word — I literally don't remember — and burn it, not a lot happened.

Many weeks later I had a very very hard conversation on the phone. It was incredibly upsetting, but I stayed still and I wrote the words of the painful call as a way of not dissociating while on the call. I later created my own burn bucket in my backyard using a cookie tin from a department store in London (Fortnum & Mason) that had previously held delicious cookies. I loaded earth into the cookie tin to weigh it down and took a lighter and lit the pieces of paper on fire and loaded them into the cookie tin burn bucket, and I burned the transcript of that call. What wasn't burned and remained, I buried in the butterfly garden directly above where Theodore's ashes are buried. b"h
